## Frequently asked questions

### What is {{APP_NAME}}?
An iPhone app that turns your selfie, or a couple or group photo, into a Halloween monster portrait with a costume, makeup and a scene, created by AI.

### Where do my photos go?
When you create a portrait, your photo and the look you picked are sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes them to a third-party AI service (OpenAI) to create the result, then returns it to your phone. We do not store your photos on our servers. According to OpenAI's published policy, data sent to its API is not used to train its models and may be kept in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days. We ask your permission before the first time a photo is sent, and you can withdraw it in Profile > Withdraw AI consent. Your finished portraits are saved on your phone; a temporary encrypted delivery copy of each new portrait is kept on our server for at most 24 hours (it is deleted as soon as your phone confirms the save, and an hourly cleanup removes it shortly after). For a before/after comparison, a resized copy of the original photo stays on your phone only; it is never uploaded. See our [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).

### Is my face used to train AI or for ads?
No. Faces and photos are used only to create the portrait you requested. We do not use them to train AI models, for advertising, or for analytics, and we do not track you.

### How do credits work?
Creations use credits: 1 credit for a portrait from one photo, 1 credit for a group portrait from one group photo, and 2 credits for a group portrait from separate selfies. Your first portrait is free (once per device). Your free first credit covers one portrait from one photo. If a creation fails or the AI service declines it, the credits are returned automatically.

### What is the difference between subscription credits and extra credit packs?
- **Subscription credits:** Starter gives 40 credits and Plus gives 100 credits every week. They reset every week when your subscription renews, and unused credits do not carry over. They are used first.
- **Extra credit packs:** 15 credits (US$3.99) or 45 credits (US$9.99). Prices shown here are US prices; the App shows the price in your local currency. These never expire, and you can buy them with or without a subscription.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose {{APP_NAME}}, and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. You keep your weekly credits until the end of the paid period. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. There is no free trial.

### How do I get a refund?
Apple handles all payments and refunds, so we cannot refund App Store purchases ourselves. Go to https://reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose Report a Problem next to the purchase, or contact Apple Support. If Apple refunds a purchase, the credits it granted may be removed.

### My purchase did not give me credits.
Open the app with an internet connection and wait a moment. Tap Restore Purchases in the Profile tab or on the Box office screen. If it still does not appear, email us with your Support ID and the Apple receipt or order number.

### How do I restore my purchases or move my credits to a new iPhone?
Open the Profile tab (or the Box office screen) and tap Restore Purchases. The app checks your purchase history with Apple and asks "Move your credits to this iPhone? They will be removed from your other install." Confirm, and your remaining subscription credits and credit pack credits move to this iPhone. They are moved, not copied, so they are no longer available on the other install. No sign-in is needed. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

### I got a new phone or reinstalled the app. Where are my credits?
If you deleted the app and installed it again on the same iPhone, your credits are usually still there: the anonymous identifier of the app is stored in the iPhone Keychain, which usually survives deleting the app (iOS does not guarantee this). Credits are not carried over automatically if you used Delete all my data, if the Keychain was cleared or did not survive (for example the iPhone was reset or erased), or on a new iPhone to which the Keychain was not restored (for example through iCloud Keychain). In those cases, if you have made a purchase, tap Restore Purchases (Profile or Box office): your active subscription and your remaining paid credits come back to that device, based on Apple's purchase records (after Delete all my data, the unused credits of your credit packs and the remaining weekly credits can be restored once, on the same Apple Account). If you never made a purchase there is nothing to restore, and the free first credit is not restored by itself. Also note that the portraits and original photos in the app's gallery are excluded from iCloud and device backups, so restoring a backup does not bring them back; save the portraits you want to keep to Photos.

### How do I delete my data?
Open Profile > Delete all my data. This removes the portraits, original photos, selfie thumbnails and AI consent on your phone and asks us to delete the anonymous record on our server (credit balance and purchase records). Your unused credit pack credits and the remaining weekly credits of an active subscription can be restored once with Restore Purchases on the same Apple Account (only their number is kept, without any link to your anonymous identifier). If Apple refunds a pack, its credits can no longer be restored. Restore returns your unused paid credits; the free first credit is not restored, so in rare cases one paid credit may be missing. Deleting the app alone does not remove the server-side record. You can also email us with your Support ID.

### How do I save or share the before/after video?
Open a finished creation on the Results screen and tap Save video to save it to Photos, or Share video to send it to another app. The video is about 4 seconds long and is made on your phone; nothing is uploaded. It needs the original photo that the App keeps with the creation for the before/after animation, which exists only for creations made from a single photo on this phone. Creations made from separate selfies, and portraits recovered after a reinstall, have no before/after video.

### Why was my photo declined or why is there an error?
Make sure the photo shows the right number of clear faces (one for solo looks, two for couples, 2 to 5 for groups) and is not too small. The AI service can decline some images or looks; you will see a message and your credits are returned. Try another photo or look. If it keeps happening, write to us.

### How do I use my privacy rights (access, deletion, objection, and so on)?
Use Profile > Delete all my data to delete your data, and Profile > Withdraw AI consent to stop photos being sent. For access, a portable copy, correction or objection, email us with your Support ID (Profile > Support ID). Your rights under GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK, LGPD, PIPA, APPI and U.S. state laws are explained in our [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).

### Can I use photos of other people?
Only add photos of people who agreed to this. Please do not upload photos of children under 13.

### Does the app work offline?
No. Creating a portrait needs an internet connection because the AI service runs online. Saved portraits can be viewed offline.

### What age is the app for?
The app is for ages 13 and up (older where the law of your country sets a higher age for consenting to the use of personal data).
