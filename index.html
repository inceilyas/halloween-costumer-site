Halloween Costumer

An iPhone app that turns your selfie, or a couple or group photo, into a Halloween monster portrait with a costume, makeup and a scene, created by AI.

Your photos

A photo is sent to a third-party AI service (OpenAI) only to create the portrait you asked for, and only after you agree in the app. We do not store the photos you send. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
